# Jim Miller

I work on software security: finding vulnerabilities, reviewing designs, and turning
research into practical guidance. I'm Engineering Director, Cryptography at
[Trail of Bits](https://trailofbits.com/team/).

My public work spans cryptographic protocols, zero-knowledge proof systems, formal
methods, and the security of machine learning software.

## Selected research and writing

- **[Weak Fiat-Shamir Attacks on Modern Proof Systems](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/691)**
  — IEEE S&P 2023. Research on missing transcript bindings and their consequences
  for deployed proof systems. Joint work with Quang Dao, Opal Wright, and Paul Grubbs.
- **[Frozen Heart disclosures](https://blog.trailofbits.com/2022/04/13/part-1-coordinated-disclosure-of-vulnerabilities-affecting-girault-bulletproofs-and-plonk/)**
  — My series explaining Trail of Bits' coordinated disclosures affecting Girault,
  Bulletproofs, and PlonK implementations.
- **[Incognito Chain and Bulletproofs](https://blog.trailofbits.com/2023/08/02/a-mistake-in-the-bulletproofs-paper-could-have-led-to-the-theft-of-millions-of-dollars/)**
  — My write-up of a vulnerability identified during our collaborative Fiat-Shamir
  research, and its remediation before publication.
- **[Assessing YOLOv7's security](https://blog.trailofbits.com/2023/11/15/assessing-the-security-posture-of-a-widely-used-vision-model-yolov7/)**
  — Threat modeling and security review of a widely used vision framework.
  Coauthored with Alvin Crighton, Anusha Ghosh, Suha Sabi Hussain, and Heidy Khlaaf.
- **[Cryptographic design review of Ockam](https://blog.trailofbits.com/2024/03/05/cryptographic-design-review-of-ockam/)**
  — Protocol design review combining manual analysis and formal methods.
  Coauthored with Joop van de Pol, Marc Ilunga, and Fredrik Dahlgren.
